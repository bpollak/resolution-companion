# Making the launch run without Brett as the bottleneck

Status: PROPOSED 2026-11-10. Nothing here is set up yet. Each access item is
Brett's to grant; each standing rule is Brett's to accept.

## Principle

Brett sets the rules once and keeps a veto. Agents work inside the rules
every day without asking. A weekly digest shows what went out and what the
numbers did. "Stop" halts everything at once.

## One-time setup (about 90 minutes of Brett's time, total)

| #   | What Brett does once                                                                                                                    | What it unlocks for agents                                                                                                                      |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Create an App Store Connect API key with the **App Manager** role                                                                       | Listing text, keywords, screenshots, the New Year product page, in-app events, release notes, and submitting builds for review, with no sign-in |
| 2   | Create a second key with the **Sales** role (or export Sales and Trends)                                                                | Installs, proceeds, and subscriptions, pulled weekly                                                                                            |
| 3   | Create brand accounts for the app on Instagram, Threads, and TikTok, and connect them to one scheduling tool that has an API            | Posts go out on a schedule from brand accounts; Brett's personal accounts are not involved                                                      |
| 4   | Create a brand mailbox, for example `hello@resolutioncompanion.com`, and grant send access                                              | Creator outreach, cohort invites and check-ins, and permission requests sent by email, with replies read and answered                           |
| 5   | Add `www` in Railway, and create a Railway token scoped to this project                                                                 | Site fixes verified and deploy health watched without him                                                                                       |
| 6   | Record one 30 to 45 minute session: the origin story, why it matters, three short clips to camera, and screen recordings of his own use | A bank of real voice, face, and footage that every draft is built from, so nothing has to be invented                                           |
| 7   | Create about 70 free-year offer codes on the annual plan                                                                                | Cohort and creator access handed out without him                                                                                                |
| 8   | Set an Apple Search Ads account with a hard monthly cap, and grant API access                                                           | Keyword bids managed inside the cap                                                                                                             |

## Standing rules (pre-approved once accepted)

Agents may, without asking each time:

- Post to the brand accounts and to X on the agreed weekly rhythm, using
  only the voice bank, real screenshots, and cohort stories that have
  written permission.
- Email creators and cohort members from the brand mailbox, signed with
  Brett's name as the founder, with free access disclosed.
- Update the store listing, product page, and in-app events, and submit them
  for review.
- Spend on Apple Search Ads inside the cap.
- Merge site copy changes that pass tests.

Every public piece passes the existing checks first: source fidelity against
the live app, the voice match, the anti-slop pass, and the claim ledger.

Agents never, under any standing rule:

- Change prices, offers, or what is free.
- Publish a testimonial without written permission, or change its meaning.
- Make health or outcome claims.
- Post to Reddit, or from Brett's personal accounts other than X.
- Spend above the cap, or pay a creator.
- Ship an app build to review that changes behavior, without Brett's yes.
- Sign in with his passkey, create accounts, accept agreements, or enter
  payment details. These stay his by design.

## What stays with Brett, and why

- The recording session and any later clips. A real face and voice is the
  thing that keeps this from reading as machine-made.
- Reddit. Subreddits ban AI-written and promotional posts; the account risk
  is his.
- App builds that change behavior, prices, and paid creator deals.
- One weekly digest, about 5 minutes: what went out, the numbers, and
  anything waiting on him.

## Trade-off to accept knowingly

Moving creator outreach from personal messages to a brand mailbox takes
Brett out of the loop, and it will likely get fewer replies than notes from
a founder's own account. The research put personal messages at roughly 10
to 25 percent replies and generic outreach under 5 percent (vendor figures).
Sending the first 10 personally, to the best-fit creators, and the rest by
email is the middle path.

## Limits worth knowing

- A live working session (like the one that wrote this) still confirms
  before it sends or submits something. Standing rules apply to the
  scheduled jobs on the Mac mini, the same way X posting already works.
- Whether a featuring nomination can be filed with an API key is not
  confirmed. If it cannot, it stays a two-minute paste for Brett.
