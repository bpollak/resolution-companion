**Description**

Resolution: Habit Tracker helps people keep a New Year's resolution by
turning it into one small daily habit, with an AI coach that builds the plan
with them and adjusts it when life gets in the way.

Version 1.7 is our New Year release, built for the weeks when people start
looking for help with their 2027 goals:

- Plan ahead. Write your plan in November or December and choose January 1
  as the start. Until then you can warm up with a two-minute version of the
  habit, and nothing counts as missed.
- A win on day one. Whatever day you set up, there is something small you
  can do right away.
- A letter to your future self. When you set your resolution, you write what
  you want to be able to say a year from now. The app gives it back to you
  at the end of the year.
- The Year You Became. A year-end look back at who you set out to become and
  the days you showed up, now free for everyone and shareable as a card.
- Kinder by design. No streak guilt. A missed day is a missed day, not a
  failure, and the two-minute version is always there.

The app is free to start, with an optional subscription. It is built and
maintained by one independent developer who made it because
no habit tracker he tried connected daily habits to the person he was trying
to become.

Priority: high. This is our main release of the year and it is timed to the
New Year's resolution season.
