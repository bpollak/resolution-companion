Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my plan?

When you first open the app, your AI coach guides you through a short conversation about who you want to become, then builds your plan: a few milestones, each with a small daily action.

Can I change my milestones or actions later?

Yes! You can edit your milestones and daily actions anytime — including which days of the week an action repeats. Premium users can also add new milestones and create additional plans.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Free includes 1 plan, 10 coaching check-ins per month, and full tracking features. Premium unlocks unlimited plans, unlimited coaching check-ins, and unlimited milestones.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime on your iPhone under Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Your personal growth data is stored locally on your device — our servers keep only an anonymous device identifier and your subscription status so purchases can be restored. AI conversations use encrypted connections and are not used to train AI models. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What is the 120 Second Kickstart?

On low-energy days, every action has a ~2-minute version you can do instead of the full thing. It still counts — showing up is what builds the habit.
