THE JANUARY PLAN

Set it up now. Start on day one.

01

Say it in your own words

Tell Coach what you want next year. "Get fit," "read more," "save money," or something only you would say. Coach turns it into one small habit with a schedule that fits your life.

02

Approve the plan before it starts

Review the suggested habit, the days it runs, and the two-minute alternative for hard days. You choose when the plan begins, even January 1, with a countdown until then.

03

One small action a day

On day one, Today shows your next step. Do the full action or its two-minute version. Both count as showing up, and one missed day never erases your record.
