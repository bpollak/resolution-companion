Description of Service

Resolution Companion is a personal development application designed to help users define personal growth goals, track daily actions, and receive AI-powered coaching. The App provides:

  • AI-assisted persona definition and goal setting
  • Daily action tracking and momentum visualization
  • AI coaching and reflection sessions
  • Progress analytics and alignment metrics
  • Optional local context tracking, personal story archives, and evidence timelines
  • Optional AI Plan Tune-Up proposals that require your confirmation before changing an action
